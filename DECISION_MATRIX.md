## Strategic Summary: Where Rules Win vs Where LLMs Earn Their Cost

1. **Where Rules Win Decisively**:
   - **Numerical, Statutory & Mathematical Logic**: Tax calculations, slab rates, marginal relief, and rounding. Rules deliver **100% accuracy**, **sub-millisecond latency**, **$0 cost**, and **complete legal defensibility**. Using an LLM for tax arithmetic is an anti-pattern.
   - **Structural Schema, Format & Boundary Invariants**: Dropped columns, date formats, coordinate bounding boxes, and foreign key constraints. Rules run at **125,000 rows/second** with zero false alarms.
2. **Where Statistical / ML Methods Win**:
   - **Distributional & Cross-Row Anomalies**: Agency null spikes and near-duplicate submissions. Neither per-row rules nor per-row LLMs can detect batch-level distribution drift. Statistical Z-scores and Locality Sensitive Hashing (LSH) operate at line-rate with zero LLM API expense.
3. **Where LLMs Genuinely Earn Their Cost**:
   - **Unstructured Natural Language Translation**: Understanding Hinglish, conversational slang, and colloquial user tax descriptions.
   - **Semantic Contradiction & Coherence Auditing**: Confirming when free-text descriptions contradict categorical metadata (e.g., ticket closed while resolution describes an active inspection), but **ONLY when gated by an upstream statistical or keyword filter** to protect budget and throughput.
