INCOME-TAX PROVISIONS FOR SALARIED INDIVIDUALS FOR FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)
Source: Income Tax Department of India & Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024

1. SCOPE AND ELIGIBILITY
Applies to individual salaried taxpayers for Assessment Year 2025-26 (Financial Year 2024-25).
Two regimes are available: Old Tax Regime and New Tax Regime under Section 115BAC(1A).
Section 115BAC is the default regime from FY 2023-24 onwards.

2. STANDARD DEDUCTION (SECTION 16(ia))
- Old Regime: A standard deduction of Rs. 50,000 or the amount of salary, whichever is less.
- New Regime (amended by Finance Act 2024): A standard deduction of Rs. 75,000 or the amount of salary, whichever is less.

3. HOUSE RENT ALLOWANCE (HRA) EXEMPTION (SECTION 10(13A) READ WITH RULE 2A)
- Applicable under Old Regime only. (Nil deduction under New Regime).
- Exemption is the least of the following three amounts:
  a) Actual HRA received from employer.
  b) Rent paid in excess of 10% of salary (Salary = Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance forming part of retirement benefits).
  c) 50% of salary if living in Metro (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) or 40% of salary in Non-Metro cities.
- If rent paid is less than or equal to 10% of salary, exemption is 0.

4. DEDUCTION FOR INTEREST ON HOME LOAN (SECTION 24(b))
- Old Regime: Interest on borrowed capital for acquisition or construction of self-occupied house property is deductible up to Rs. 2,00,000 against salary income.
- New Regime: Section 24(b) deduction for self-occupied property is not allowed, and house property loss cannot be set off against salary income.

5. CHAPTER VI-A DEDUCTIONS (APPLICABLE UNDER OLD REGIME ONLY)
- Section 80C: Deductions for eligible investments (EPF, PPF, ELSS, life insurance premiums, home loan principal repayment, etc.) capped at an aggregate maximum of Rs. 1,50,000.
- Section 80CCD(1B): Additional deduction for voluntary contribution to National Pension System (NPS) Tier-I account up to Rs. 50,000.
- Section 80D: Health insurance premium and preventive health check-up:
  a) Self, spouse, and dependent children: Max Rs. 25,000 (Rs. 50,000 if taxpayer is senior citizen aged 60 or above).
  b) Parents: Max Rs. 25,000 (Rs. 50,000 if parents are senior citizens aged 60 or above).
  c) Preventive health check-up is included within the overall limit, up to Rs. 5,000.
  d) Maximum allowable under 80D is Rs. 1,00,000 (if both taxpayer and parents are senior citizens), or Rs. 75,000 (non-senior taxpayer + senior parents).
- None of Section 80C, 80CCD(1B), or 80D is allowable under the New Regime.

6. TAX SLABS & RATES FOR FY 2024-25

A. OLD REGIME (For individuals aged under 60 years):
- Up to Rs. 2,50,000: Nil
- Rs. 2,50,001 to Rs. 5,00,000: 5% of amount exceeding Rs. 2,50,000
- Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000: Rs. 12,500 + 20% of amount exceeding Rs. 5,00,000
- Above Rs. 10,00,000: Rs. 1,12,500 + 30% of amount exceeding Rs. 10,00,000
(For senior citizens aged 60 to 79: First slab is up to Rs. 3,00,000 Nil; for super senior aged 80+: First slab is up to Rs. 5,00,000 Nil).

B. NEW REGIME (Section 115BAC as revised by Finance (No. 2) Act 2024):
- Up to Rs. 3,00,000: Nil
- Rs. 3,00,001 to Rs. 7,00,000: 5% of amount exceeding Rs. 3,00,000
- Rs. 7,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000: Rs. 20,000 + 10% of amount exceeding Rs. 7,00,000
- Rs. 10,00,001 to Rs. 12,00,000: Rs. 50,000 + 15% of amount exceeding Rs. 10,00,000
- Rs. 12,00,001 to Rs. 15,00,000: Rs. 80,000 + 20% of amount exceeding Rs. 12,00,000
- Above Rs. 15,00,000: Rs. 1,40,000 + 30% of amount exceeding Rs. 15,00,000

7. REBATE UNDER SECTION 87A AND MARGINAL RELIEF
- Old Regime: If Total Income does not exceed Rs. 5,00,000, rebate of 100% of income tax or Rs. 12,500, whichever is less. (Tax liability is Nil up to Rs. 5,00,000). No marginal relief in Old Regime.
- New Regime:
  a) If Total Income does not exceed Rs. 7,00,000, rebate of 100% of income tax (up to Rs. 25,000). Tax liability is Nil.
  b) Marginal Relief on Section 87A: If Total Income slightly exceeds Rs. 7,00,000, the income-tax payable shall not exceed the amount by which Total Income exceeds Rs. 7,00,000 (i.e. Tax <= Total Income - 7,00,000). This provides relief for incomes between Rs. 7,00,001 and Rs. 7,27,778.

8. SURCHARGE AND SURCHARGE MARGINAL RELIEF
Applicable on income tax before cess:
- Total Income Rs. 50,00,001 to Rs. 1,00,00,000: 10% surcharge.
  Marginal Relief: Total tax + surcharge shall not exceed Tax on Rs. 50,00,000 + (Total Income - Rs. 50,00,000).
- Total Income Rs. 1,00,00,001 to Rs. 2,00,00,000: 15% surcharge.
  Marginal Relief: Total tax + surcharge shall not exceed (Tax + Surcharge on Rs. 1,00,00,000) + (Total Income - Rs. 1,00,00,000).
- Total Income Rs. 2,00,00,001 to Rs. 5,00,00,000: 25% surcharge.
  Marginal Relief: Total tax + surcharge shall not exceed (Tax + Surcharge on Rs. 2,00,00,000) + (Total Income - Rs. 2,00,00,000).
- Total Income above Rs. 5,00,00,000:
  Old Regime: 37% surcharge with marginal relief against Rs. 5,00,00,000.
  New Regime: Capped at 25% surcharge (Finance Act 2023 onwards).

9. HEALTH AND EDUCATION CESS
A cess of 4% is levied on the aggregate of income-tax and surcharge, after deducting Section 87A rebate.

10. ROUNDING OFF (SECTIONS 288A & 288B)
- Total Taxable Income is rounded off to the nearest multiple of Rs. 10 under Section 288A.
- Total Tax Payable (including surcharge and cess) is rounded off to the nearest multiple of Rs. 10 under Section 288B.
